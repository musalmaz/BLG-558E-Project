Takeaway: a shared memory leaks private data to other users even with a “do not reveal” rule. Per-user isolation removed it. Behavioral side channels: only 1 of 24 decoder cells passed the threshold (p ≈ 0.03), weak under multiple comparisons.

50 probes per cell on topics where A shared data; with no shared data the leakage rate is 0. Isolation is enforced at retrieval; user identity is given by the setup (authentication is not modeled).

BLG558E · Security & Privacy · Final Project

Leakage is measured as the share of B's replies that contain a secret marker from A's private note, on the topics where A actually shared something. X1 and X3 leak 52 to 72 percent even though the prompt forbids it. X2 is at the noise floor: the one or two hits for gemma3 are numbers that appear by coincidence. When A shared nothing on a topic, nothing leaks in any mode. We did not model authentication; the identity of B comes from the experiment setup, so a real deployment depends on correct identity tagging.
