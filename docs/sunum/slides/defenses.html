BLG558E · Security & Privacy · Final Project

A defense that destroys usefulness is not a defense, so every defense is scored on two axes: the decoder AUC (security) and the answer quality on a utility set (resolution rate, routing agreement, marker recall). Output-side fixes such as normalizing refusals or hiding decisions do nothing for the text channel, because the signal is the style of ordinary answers. Scoped context closes the channel but loses all internal-source facts; M2 gives the same security at a fraction of the cost, its price being a lower resolution rate for gemma3 (0.92 to 0.67).
