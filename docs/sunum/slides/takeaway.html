Conclusions

1. With a weak ACL the secret content leaks directly; a strong ACL (filter before the model) closes it.

2. The agent’s decision fields were not a reliable side channel here; the reply style was (AUC 0.82).

3. Only keeping confidential text out of the context closed the style channel. Masking removes content but not the topic’s existence.

4. Shared conversation memory leaks across users; per-user isolation removes it.

Future work: more topics and repetitions, a commercial model, other secret policies, isolation with real authentication.

Thank you · Questions?

Practical advice: enforce access control before the model, not in the prompt; do not rely on post-hoc output fixes or on masking alone when even the existence of a topic is sensitive; keep conversation memory separated per user.
