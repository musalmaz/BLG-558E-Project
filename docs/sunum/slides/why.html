Finding 2: the signal comes from writing style, not from words

We masked topic words, numbers, proper names and 210 words that appear only in confidential documents, then re-ran the decoder. AUC did not drop (0.83 → 0.82).

BLG558E · Security & Privacy · Final Project

We first suspected the text signal was just secret words leaking into replies. Masking disproved that. The difference is stylistic: when confidential material is in context the model writes more like an insider, using we and our, apologizes less, and does not send people to a phone number or email. Topic-level tests give p of about 0.0006 for we/our and for I, but this is an exploratory analysis without correction.
